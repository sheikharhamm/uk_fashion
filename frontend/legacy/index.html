Centuries of Heritage

The Living Art of Handwoven Balochi Doch

Every bridal dress presented at UK Fashion is an authentic cultural treasure. Unlike mass-manufactured bridal suits, true Balochi doch takes between 3 to 6 months of painstaking hand embroidery.

Unmatched Artisanal Precision

Stitched by generational Balochi women artisans using needlepoint geometric perfection.

Pure Silks & Precious Threads

Katan silk, micro velvet 9000, real gold tilla, mukaish, and resham threads that never lose lustre.
